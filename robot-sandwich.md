# Robot makes a PB & J

## Assumptions

- We have a fully charge robot with empty hands
- There is a counter with the following items on it:
    - Jar of PB
    - Jar of Jelly
    - Loaf or wrapped bread
    - Butter Knife
    - Plate
    - Napkin

<br>

## Steps to Follow
1. Unwrap the bread, remove two slices, place on plate side by side
2. Open the PB & Jelly jars
3. Grab the butter knife in one hand and the PB in the other
4. Use the knife to sccop out the PB, set down the jar
5. Pick up slice of bread and spread PB over the flat surface
6. Put bread with PB back on the plate, clean side down
7. Pick up wet napkin wipe excess PB off from knife, set down napkin
8. Pick up Jelly, use knife to scoop jelly, set down the jar
9. Pick up slice of bread without PB on it
10. Spread the jelly over flat surface
11. Place the "jelly bread" on top of the "PB bread" jelly side down
12. Put lids back on both jars, throw away the napkin, put knife in sink, put ingredients away

## Exit conditions
- Constructed sandwich and plate are the only items on the table
